# Acme Workspace Pricing

Acme Workspace is billed per workspace, per month. All plans include unlimited viewers and a 14-day free trial.

## Starter plan

The Starter plan costs $12 per month for up to 5 team members. It includes 10 active projects, 5 GB of file storage,
basic reporting and email support with a one business day response time. Starter does not include single sign-on,
audit logs or the public API.

## Team plan

The Team plan costs $49 per month for up to 25 team members. It includes unlimited projects, 100 GB of file storage,
advanced reporting, the public API with 10,000 requests per day, and priority chat support. Team adds role-based
permissions so owners and admins can control who can invite members and create projects.

## Enterprise plan

The Enterprise plan is priced by contract. It includes single sign-on (SAML), audit logs with 2 years of retention,
a dedicated success manager, a 99.95% uptime SLA and custom data residency. Contact sales to start an Enterprise trial.

## Billing and invoices

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing cycle and are available on the Billing page. An invoice is marked
Overdue when payment has not been received 7 days after the due date. Overdue projects keep working for 14 days, after
which the workspace becomes read-only until the balance is paid.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded but can be cancelled at
any time from the Billing page.
