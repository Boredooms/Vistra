Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme Workspace. This guide covers the first steps for new workspace owners.

Invite a teammate

Open the Team page from the sidebar, choose Invite member, enter the teammate's name and email address, and send the invitation. The teammate receives an email and appears in the list as Invited until they accept. Only owners and admins can invite members.

Create your first project

Open the Projects page and choose New project. Give the project a name of up to 80 characters. Projects start in the Active status and are listed with their owner and status.

Understanding project statuses

  • Active: the project is in use and its workspace is in good standing.
  • Overdue: the workspace has an unpaid invoice that is past its due date. Pay the invoice on the Billing page to clear it.
  • Archived: the project is hidden from the default list and is read-only.

Security basics

Turn on two-factor authentication under Settings. Workspace owners can require it for every member on the Team plan and above.
